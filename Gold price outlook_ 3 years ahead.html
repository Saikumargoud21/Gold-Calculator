What does the 80% swing mean?

The swing sets the scale of the whole model. Your slider scores and the weights only decide what share of the swing you get. For example, if every factor scores +5, you get the full swing. If real rates, the dollar and central banks are all positive but the rest are neutral, you get only about 55% of it (25 + 15 + 15 weights out of 100).

I chose 80% because gold has had 3-year gains of 50% or more in strong bull runs, and falls of 30 to 40% in weak periods. A fully positive case is rare, so 80% is a deliberately wide ceiling.

Suggested settings: 40 to 50 for a cautious view, 80 for the default, 100 or more only for an aggressive view. The model is symmetric, so a fully negative case also falls by the same percentage, which is harsher than gold’s usual downside. If you think downside is smaller, lower the swing or use milder negative scores.
